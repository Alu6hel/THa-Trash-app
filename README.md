OverviewChoreOgraph is an offline-first behavioral design engine disguised as a task manager. Relying on sheer willpower to get through daily chores leads to burnout. Instead, ChoreOgraph utilizes the psychological principle of "habit stacking", where you attach a new or difficult habit to an established, automatic one (the anchor). By seamlessly bridging your morning, afternoon, and evening routines, the app removes decision fatigue and helps you feel accomplished before you even start your workday.  Core Features (The Daily Flow)The Morning Launchpad: Stop deciding what to do first. Set an "Anchor Task" (like brewing coffee) and let the app build your sequence. Example Stack: Make the bed → Personal hygiene → Brew coffee → Prepare breakfast & pack lunches → Feed pets → Review the daily schedule while drinking coffee.The Afternoon Shift (Remote Work Integration): Built for the modern hybrid worker. The app pairs passive household chores with active work blocks. It reminds you to load the dishwasher during your 5-minute screen break, or rotate the laundry right after logging out for the day, keeping your living space tidy without interrupting deep work.The Evening Wind-Down: A dedicated protocol to close out the day and prep for the next. The app gently guides the transition from high-energy tasks (helping with homework, cooking, eating dinner) into leisure (TV, social media scrolling). Finally, it triggers a "Prep & Rest" sequence: setting out clothes, completing evening hygiene, and reading to ensure a clean break before sleep.Micro-Rewards & Streaks: The brain needs immediate positive reinforcement. Ticking off simple tasks like "Checking the mail" or "Wiping the counters" triggers subtle UI celebrations that build dopamine-driven momentum.Map Your RoutineVisualizing your day makes it easier to execute. Explore how tasks link together to form a seamless daily flow:Key insight: The most successful routines don't rely on strict timestamps. They rely on triggers. When "finishing dinner" automatically triggers "wiping the counters," the friction of starting the chore disappears.Technical ArchitectureTo ensure your home routines and personal schedules remain entirely private, ChoreOgraph is designed with a localized, privacy-first infrastructure.Core Backend: The logic engine and routine schedulers are written in Rust and Zig, guaranteeing memory safety, concurrency, and lightning-fast execution without draining background battery life.Intelligent Suggestions: The app uses an embedded Ollama infrastructure to run quantized local language models. It analyzes your completed tasks and automatically suggests optimized chore sequences (e.g., suggesting you pull the meat out of the freezer during your morning coffee sequence) without sending your routine data to a cloud server.Storage: Utlizes a local, encrypted SQLite database. Your schedule and household data never leave the hardware.
